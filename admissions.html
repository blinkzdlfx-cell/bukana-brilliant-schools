Admissions

We welcome enquiries from families who share our commitment to education and character. The admission process is designed to be supportive and transparent.

Why Apply

A school that cares about every child.

At Bukana Brilliant Schools, admission isn't just about academic potential — it's about finding the right fit for each child and family. We value character, curiosity, and a willingness to learn alongside academic ability.

Our admissions team is available to answer questions, arrange school visits, and guide you through every step of the process.

Admission requirements and availability are subject to confirmation by the school.

Process

Admission Process

Four simple steps from enquiry to enrolment.

  1. Enquiry

    Contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp to ask questions and express interest.

  2. School Visit / Assessment

    Book a tour and, where applicable, arrange an age-appropriate assessment or observation.

  3. Admission Decision

    Receive a thoughtful decision based on your child's needs and available capacity.

  4. Enrolment

    Complete the enrolment forms and your child joins our school community.

What You'll Need

Typical Requirements

The following is general guidance. Please contact the school for confirmed requirements for each stage.

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or proof of age
  • Previous school records (if applicable)
  • Immunisation records
  • Parent/guardian identification
  • Additional documents as requested

All requirements are general guidance and subject to confirmation by the school.
