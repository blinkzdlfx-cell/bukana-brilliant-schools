WHAT TO PREPARE

Admission requirements.

The final list should be confirmed by the school for each class and admission session. Typical requirements may include:

Child information

Basic details, date of birth and previous school information where applicable.

Parent / guardian details

Contact information and relevant emergency contact details.

Academic records

Previous reports, assessment records or transfer information where required.

Supporting documents

Any additional documents requested by the school during enrolment.
