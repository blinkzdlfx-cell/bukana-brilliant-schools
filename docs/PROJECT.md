# Project Definition — Bukana Brilliant Schools

## 1. Identity

- **Official name:** Bukana Brilliant Schools
- **Short identity:** BBS
- **Tagline:** Be a star @ BBS
- **Domain:** bukanabrilliantschools.com
- **Location:** Degbuyi Complex, 282, Jegun/Ikotun Road, Thomas Adeoye B/Stop, Ijegun, Lagos, Nigeria
- **Educational levels:** Creche, Nursery, Primary

The school name, tagline, educational levels, domain, and address above are the currently supplied project details. Other claims require owner verification.

## 2. Purpose

Create a clear, visually distinctive, trustworthy school website that introduces Bukana Brilliant Schools to parents and guardians, explains its educational offering, presents school life, and makes it easy to enquire about admissions or contact the school.

The site should help a first-time visitor answer:
1. What is Bukana Brilliant Schools?
2. Which age/education levels does it serve?
3. What is the school's educational approach?
4. What does the learning environment look and feel like?
5. How can a parent learn about admission?
6. How can a parent contact or locate the school?

## 3. Audience

### Primary
Parents and guardians considering Creche, Nursery, or Primary education.

### Secondary
Current parents, family members, prospective staff, and community visitors looking for verified school information.

### Audience needs
- Clear navigation and readable information.
- Confidence that the school is real and contact information is official.
- Simple explanation of the educational levels.
- Relevant photographs and a sense of school life.
- A clear admissions enquiry path.
- Easy access to location and contact information.

## 4. Experience principles

- **Trust before hype:** factual, useful, verifiable information.
- **Warmth through imagery:** use relevant, approved school and learning imagery.
- **Clarity:** explain school levels and next steps in plain language.
- **Distinctiveness:** a dark monochrome visual foundation with expressive typography and intentional contrast.
- **Accessibility:** readable, keyboard-friendly, mobile-first experience.
- **Performance:** fast initial load, optimized images, minimal dependencies.
- **Maintainability:** reusable components and structured content.

## 5. Brand and visual direction

The site uses a dark monochrome foundation:
- Near-black background.
- White primary text and key actions.
- Muted grey supporting text.
- Dark grey surfaces.
- Subtle borders and restrained highlights.

Avoid a white-template appearance. The school context should come through through photography, copy, layout, and purposeful visual storytelling—not by abandoning the chosen palette.

The visual tone should be confident, welcoming, contemporary, safe, and appropriate for early-years and primary education.

## 6. Core website experience

The initial public website is expected to include:
- Home
- About the School
- Academics / Learning Levels
- Admissions
- School Life / Gallery
- News & Events (only when verified content is available)
- Contact / Find Us

The exact route map and required page sections are detailed in `SITE_SPEC.md`.

## 7. Product boundary

Initial release is a public-facing informational and enquiry website.

No student information system, learning management system, parent portal, staff portal, payment processing, attendance system, database-backed CMS, authentication, or administrative dashboard is included in the initial scope.

Any future feature that collects or stores personal information requires a separate privacy, security, data-retention, and backend design review.

## 8. Success criteria

A successful first release:
- Explains the school and its three educational levels.
- Has a coherent and memorable homepage.
- Makes admissions and contact actions easy to find.
- Uses only confirmed school information.
- Looks intentional on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Loads quickly and behaves accessibly.
- Has working routes, metadata, and deployment configuration.
- Can be maintained without editing repeated content in multiple components.
